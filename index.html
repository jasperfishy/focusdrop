Get FocusDrop on your devices

Installs like an app, opens instantly, works offline, and your thoughts stay on that device.

Download offline copy

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Android phone & tablet

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install now above, or the ⋮ menu then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Windows, Mac & Linux

  1. In Chrome or Edge, click Install now above, or the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  2. In Safari on Mac, use File > Add to Dock.

The offline copy is a single file you can open in any browser. It keeps its own list, separate from the installed app. Use Export and Import in Settings to move your thoughts between them.
